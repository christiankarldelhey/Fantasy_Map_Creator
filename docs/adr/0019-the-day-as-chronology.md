# The day as chronology: one TODAY skeleton, no phase banners

The narrator prompt used to deliver the day as three labelled phase blocks (`=== MORNING ===`, `=== AFTERNOON ===`, `=== NIGHT AT CAMP ===`) of "reference only" fragments — weather, terrain, locations, encounters grouped by `when.phase` — and asked the model to narrate them in order. The representation did not embody the order it demanded: `when.hour` was dropped at the boundary, so a 16:00 exchange could surface at midday; place sightings, pass-throughs and the night's rest competed as equally-weighted notes with no stated spine; a fixed `NIGHT AT CAMP` banner primed a wild camp on inn nights; and the same cold-rain fact reached the prompt through as many as eight channels (climate state, lands character, one weather line per phase, the rest reading, the lens, the Focus), which read as eight narrative reasons to keep writing mud — indoors included.

The day now reaches the narrator as a single `=== TODAY ===` skeleton (ADR-0016's events, the day['encounters'] channel and the phase-keyed stirs merged into one ordered beat list). The previous Episode's rest reading opens the skeleton — "The day begins — …" — so where the traveller woke is a stated fact, not a deduction. Beats sort by `(phase, hour)`; clock hours never reach the prompt — `day_time_phrase` speaks them naturally ("late in the afternoon", never "16:00"), anchored on fixed `DAWN_HOUR = 7` / `DUSK_HOUR = 19` until a seasonal daylight engine replaces the table in one place. Weather is told whole once; a later climate event re-enters only as the clauses that changed ("Later — rain begins"), so an unchanging sky earns one line and a real shift keeps its narrative weight. The stirs keep their C21/C35 placement — the Focus and what the day stirs land at the end of their own stretch of the timeline, echoes still ride inside the encounter that called them up — and two rules close the skeleton: encounters happen only where the timeline puts them, and only the places named exist. The stateless path (no `events`) renders the same skeleton from the `world_notes` phase buckets in canonical order.

## Consequences

- `phase_block` and the phase banners die; `=== TODAY ===` is the skeleton — headings exist in the prompt only, never in the prose.
- `_encounter_prompt_shape` keeps `when.hour`; beats without one recover their position from the time phrase their own reading already carries (`implied_hour` — the first time word in the text anchors it, sort-only). An event with neither phase nor implied hour is day-level material and opens the skeleton rather than falling into the night bucket.
- `TODAY'S WAY IN` is gone: the prescribed sensory opening produced the same tic every chapter; the dawn anchor gives the opening its shape instead.
- The night's rest is a beat like any other — a bought bed reads as an inn, a hedge as open country; no banner prejudges it.
- Terminal days emit no night beats and the death-notice no longer references a block that is not there.
- `previous_rest` threads `narrate_episode` → `narrate_day` → `build_day_prompt`; only a literally consecutive `episode_date` counts as "yesterday".
- Verified against real episodes: the day-4 chapter now narrates the Pony wake-up, the mid-morning watchers, the late-afternoon exchange and the Shire camp in order — no invented departures, no mud in the common room.
