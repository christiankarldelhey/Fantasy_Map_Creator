# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Mind Engine](./CONTEXT.md): the inner life of a Character — memories, beliefs, needs, mood, and how an Episode was lived
- [Game World](./backend/CONTEXT.md): the host world the traveller journeys through — map, calendar, encounters, and the Movers that cross it

## Relationships

- **Game World → Mind Engine**: the world reports the Condition snapshot and the Events of each Episode (Crossings and Traces among them); the Mind Engine decides how they were lived and narrated
